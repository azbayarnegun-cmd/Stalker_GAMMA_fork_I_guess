SA Rigs, Belts & Pouches v1.0.2

Mandatory equipment/content package. Requires SA Inventory Framework &
Compatibility v1.0.1 and loads after it.

INCLUDED
  five modular belts with one to five attachment points
  all sixteen rebalanced chest-rig layouts with two addon-pouch fittings each
  G1-G4 utility, Y1-Y5 ammunition and R1-R2 medical rig pouches
  Layered magazine, grenade, medical, administrative, universal, dump, parts,
  Hunter, Sustainment and Canister pouch families
  wide-first Sustainment grids: 2x1, 2x2, 3x2 and 4x2
  Canister carriers: 3x1 and 3x2
  belt fitting rules and grenade-pouch mine/IED acceptance
  yellow, green and red pocket tags and physical storage rules

Hunter Kits accept raw mutant meat, parts, hides and organs. Cooked mutant food
belongs in Sustainment. Hunter, Sustainment and Canister pouches are backpack-
only. This package defines physical equipment; exact prices, recipes, trader
stock and stacking are supplied by the optional Equipment Balance package.

V1.0.1 FIXES
  belt descriptions now match the actual one-to-five-slot ladder
  every chest rig now exposes exactly two Layered addon-pouch fittings

V1.0.2 SUSTAINMENT VISIBILITY FIX
  rebases Sustainment T1 on the visible Small Universal Pouch item class
  makes T2-T4 inherit progressively from the preceding Sustainment tier
  removes inherited Hunter Kit UI classification from the Sustainment family
  preserves provisions-only routing, backpack-only mounting, section IDs,
  capacities, prices, names and save compatibility

Replace v1.0.1 with v1.0.2. Do not enable both versions.
v1.0.2 Utility Pouches

Adds two strict Universal Pouch derivatives:
- Field Utility Pouch: 2x2 internal grid, 5,000 RU.
- Extended Utility Pouch: 3x2 internal grid, 10,000 RU.

Both accept knives, multitools, Swiss knives, bolts and matches only, and
use the same outfit/rig/belt/backpack mounting compatibility as the Universal
Pouch. Use with 30_SA_Equipment_Balance_v1.0.3_Utility_Pouches for trader and
crafting integration.
