SA Combat Access & QuickWheel v1.0.0

Optional quick-access package. Requires the consolidated Framework and
Equipment packages, Quick Action Wheel - HarukaSai, and Toxic Air v2 Redux.

PAGE 1
  consumables, grenades and loose mask filters physically accessible from the
  worn rig. The installed Toxic Air filter appears as an unequip action.

PAGE 2
  magazines physically stored in active rig reload pockets. Dump-pouch,
  backpack, belt, outfit and ordinary inventory contents are excluded.

Manual Add to Wheel and manual removal remain disabled. Food, drinks,
cigarettes, devices and medicine follow the rig quick-use discipline controlled
by SquaredAway's existing rig-only MCM setting.

This package deliberately does not contain zzz_amp_layers.script. SmartLoot and
filter return routing are owned by the consolidated Framework, preventing the
QuickWheel addon from overwriting trade or pouch registration fixes.
