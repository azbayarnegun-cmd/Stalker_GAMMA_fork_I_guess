SquaredAway 3.1.0-test compatibility port

Load after SquaredAway 3.1.0-test and the installed Quick Action Wheel files.
This addon replaces the two SquaredAway scripts that require direct Layered integration:
- gamedata/scripts/zzz_amp_grid.script
- gamedata/scripts/zzz_armor_mag_pouches.script

All other Layered Pouches 1.5.3 assets/configs/scripts are included.
The tested QAW compatibility script is included so separate rig-use/QAW
hotfixes are no longer required.
